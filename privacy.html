Privacy Notice

Print Farm Scheduler · internal application

Print Farm Scheduler is an internal tool for scheduling and tracking 3D print jobs. It is available only to people inside this organization who already have access to the team SharePoint site. This notice describes what the application does with information.

What the application stores

The application stores the operational records you enter into it: printer names and configurations, print job details (title, quantity, priority, expected completion, file path, slicing and print settings), and the names typed into the "sent by" and "give to" fields of a job.

These records are written to SharePoint lists on your organization's own SharePoint site. They are not sent anywhere else.

Sign-in

Signing in uses your existing work account through Microsoft Entra ID. The application receives an access token that lets it read and write the SharePoint lists on your behalf. It does not see or store your password. The token is held in your browser for the length of your session and is discarded when the session ends.

What the application does not do

  • It does not sell, share, or transfer information to third parties.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • It does not set cookies beyond what sign-in requires.
  • It does not collect information about you beyond your account identity and what you type into the board.

Who can see the information

Everyone with access to the underlying SharePoint site can see the full board, including any names entered on a job. Access is governed entirely by the permissions on that site; the application maintains no separate account list or permission system of its own.

Retention and deletion

Records persist until someone deletes them — by removing a job, printer, or group in the application, or by editing the SharePoint lists directly. Deletions made in the application are permanent from the application's point of view. Recovery, backup, and retention are governed by your organization's SharePoint configuration, not by this tool.

Third-party components

The application runs in your browser and loads its supporting libraries (React, Microsoft Authentication Library, and interface components) from public content delivery networks. Those services receive the standard request information any website visit involves, such as your IP address. They receive none of your print job data.

Questions

This is an internally built tool. Direct questions about it, or requests to correct or remove information, to the team that maintains it or to your systems administrator.

This notice describes an internal application and supplements, rather than replaces, your organization's own privacy and data handling policies. Where the two differ, your organization's policies govern.
